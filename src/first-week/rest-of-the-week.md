# Progression goals

## Build for PvE as well as PvP.

- **Prioritize** Attack Power → Accuracy for PvE.
- A PvP-only gear setup will hold back your PvE damage.
- Build a PvE set for Sanctuary **first**, then invest in a separate PvP set; **<u>PvP gear underperforms in PvE</u>**.

## Earn Kinah

- Run higher-tier dungeons, farm open-world item drops, and sell unbound yellow items to fund crafted PvE gear.
- Open-world farming is limited to 1 million Kinah a day; Value here isn't the Kinah drop itself, but the item drops.
- Abyss farming has no daily Kinah cap.
- PvP Daeva board points are worth a LOT. Selling what you get from the Battlefield will get you more progression in Kinah vs. what you’d get from using the points yourself. Simple cost-benefit analysis.

## Build lasting PvP pieces

- Get your PvP weapon early; it’s worth enhancing beyond +10.
- Get blue Paralyze and Stun theostones (Blue ones are bis for a while). Socket one into a weapon, and the other in a guard (make sure to not put it in a guard that you will end up replacing).
- Spend early Abyss points on stigmas before committing heavily to PvP gear.

## Reach Sanctuary Raid

- Build your crafted PvE setup and aim for a <u>2700</u> gear score.
- **Keep** +10 T3 Ferocious Den gear. If you need its displayed score to meet the entry requirement; swap to stronger crafted pieces inside if appropriate.
- Once Sanctuary is on farm, put more resources into PvP gear.

## Upgrade key skills

- Aim for **+16** on your top two or three skills.
- Get **+14** through the Daeva Board, then seek the remaining **+2** from skill bonuses on rings or Arcana cards (RNG based).

## Do the first-week statue objective

- Kill Conquest Draupnir’s final boss **10 times** for the stat-granting statue. **<u>Skip side bosses with a group and don’t collect rewards.</u>** This is separate from farming T1 Draupnir for loot.
