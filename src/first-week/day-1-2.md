# DAY 1 - 2

### 1) Do the main story quest (MSQ) to level up to 45

Do all side quests + sealed dungeons along the path of quests

Get 21 Feathers for the Movement Speed tilte\
Get 27 Feathers for Blue Belt and Amulet

ALL DUNGEONS IN MSQ CAN BE SOLOED. <u>**DO NOT CLAIM THE REWARDS HERE**</u>. DO NOT NEED TO PARTY.

---

### 2) Get 1000 gear score to get an MSQ yellow weapon

To get this fast, get yourself 2x runes. Each increases gear score by 40.

<u>**THESE RUNES CAN BLOW UP IF YOU UPGRADE THEM. DON'T GO PAST +1 FOR NOW.**</u>

Also, getting your pilgrim accessories will help you get an easy gear score at this point.

---

### 3) Do all sealed dungeons on Asmodian side + all sealed dungeons on Elyos side (2-3 rifts in total) = around 1650 gear score then all Abyss Feathers

A lot of people will miss the importance of this. You’ll be fighting people a week later who still haven't done all their sealed dungeons because they didn't know how important it is.

Do all Feathers in the Abyss Early so don’t need to later do this Day 1 or 2. People are very undergeared Day 1 to 2 so there damage is low making it hard for you to die.

---

## <u>IMPORTANT TIPS</u>

- **DO NOT** do the T1 conquest dungeons.
  - **Skip** Draupnir + Krao cave completely. Save your energy for better rewards.
- **Save your Energy** for **T2+** dungeons. **1500 gearscore = T2 dungeons**
  - Vakron is one to focus on. Do “exploration” Vakron to get the pity piece. Then you can do the conquest version with a group.
  - The objective is to get as high a gear score as possible to do higher-tier dungeons. Energy is limited, so spend it on the highest/most difficult content ASAP for better rewards and progression.
- **Prioritize damage early.** Stack Attack Power and enough Accuracy to handle enemies that block. Add defense when you need it later on.
  - All content is usually gated by DPS checks. If you have hands and not a retard, most damage is avoidable in PvE content, making DPS the real check.
- **Prioritize pet progression.** It’s a valuable long-term grind.
  - Choose pet rewards from daily dungeons, and farm mobs when you have spare time.
- **Always buy and use cheap scrolls and power shards.** They’re worth the cost.
- **Limit investment in dungeon gear.**
  - Stop MSQ and <u>pre-dungeon gear</u> at **+5**, and <u>dungeon gear</u> at **+10**.
    - Crafted PvE gear & PvP gear can transfer their progression to the next tier for a little amount of $$.
    - <u>**Dungeon gear DOES NOT have this feature to transfer to a new piece of Dungeon gear.**</u>
    - Every piece of dungeon gear you enhance is a sunk cost. +10 is fairly cheap; past that, the cost goes up a lot.
  - Save stat-reroll materials (yellows + books) to reroll actual gear you’ll use long-term.
  - <u>**DO NOT REROLL STATS ON MSQ/DUNGEON GEAR**</u>
- **Enchant runes cautiously.** A failed enchant destroys the rune. Get +1, then wait until you have backups.
- **Keep dungeon gear for gear-score requirements.** Crafted PvE gear can be stronger despite displaying a lower gear score. Use dungeon gear to get the required score, then swap into crafted gear after you’re inside the dungeon.
- **When you have money, craft lasting PvE gear in this order:** *accessories → weapon and guard → armor.*
  - On pieces you’ll keep, **prioritize** *weapon/guard* traits (Might, Combat Speed, Weapon Damage Amplification), *glove* Attack Speed, *boot* Movement Speed, and *ring skill bonuses*.
- **Daeva Board:**
  - **Prioritize** orange damage nodes, then bonuses for active skills you use, then Attack nodes.
  - At level 45, the board alone can bring those skills to level 14.
- **Feathers:**
  - Collect them early and complete the reward tracks.
  - **Finish Abyss feathers around days 2–3:** 100% collection grants 45 PvP Daeva Board points. <u>**DO THIS ASAP, OTHERWISE IT WILL BE HARD TO COLLECT LATER ON**</u>
