## Macros

- Do keep a solo keybind for Dark Crush in scenarios where you want to spam while kiting away to handle mechanics or otherwise
- If you use Fracturing Blow, you'll typically want to use it right before Marchutan's to line up the shred with your burst window, although in general whether you want to use skills that aren't mobile and gap-closers in your macro is up to you
- Korean Advice for macro setup:
  - Make sure to set your LMB/RMB to a keyboard key for instance:
    - “LMB” is “[“
    - “RMB” is “]”
    - For me I also made “E” secondary “-” as well
    - And then also in-game macro bind I set to “\\”
  - Setup macro on mouse software to push down and hold “[“, “\\” and “-”
    - ![Chanter macro screenshot 1](../images/classes/chanter/macro-1.png)
    - This setup works best for me, but play around and find what works best for you
    - I personally setup this macro to when I hold my right click and I rebind my right click in-game so I can still use it manually when I want to (Do what works for you)
  - So you have the mouse software macro holding down the in-game macro (Just Dark Crush), your “LMB” which is “[“ and your “E” which is also “-”

## PvP Setup

- For PvP you can follow this questlog for prio on stats for gear/title/arcana/wings
  - <https://questlog.gg/aion-2/en/character-builder/SacredJuniorOfTheFlame>
- Below is a skill set and priority, main thing here is getting **Machutan’s Wrath** Stigma skill to level 15 for the healing reduce and for Active skills you want to get your main CC skills to at least 8-12 (**Heat Wave Blow**, **Tremor Crush**, **Wave Blow**)
- The next important priority would be the **Focused Defense** Stigma skill for the block/parry against CC if timed correctly you get 2 charges at level 5 and its mobile at 10
- I will make a video later on PvP comboing but there are many routes to take, the main thing is baiting out the users **Defiance** (Tenacity) before using your burst. Typically we can sacrifice **Tremor Crush** or **Wave Blow** to bait out a **Defiance**. Once popped usually you can use your **Focused Defense** to block the counter CC and hold your **Defiance**. If no **Defiance** is baited just throw burst into the target. You also can save **Heat Wave Blow** for **Seal** on the target to avoid any burst or secure a kill.
- Macros don’t change from PvE but you might want to remove E from the macro so its just LMB/RMB and you use E when you want to burst (Note: **Machutan** procs your **Dark Crush** so you want to space out your **Spinning Strike** and Machutan for burst especially if they run its all range hits)
